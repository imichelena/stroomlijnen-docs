# Compatible Vehicles

Plug & Charge compatibility depends on the vehicle's support for **ISO 15118-2** with contract certificate handling. Below is the current status for vehicles commonly seen at Stroomlijnen locations.

:::info Updated March 2026
Compatibility is evolving rapidly. We update this page as OEMs release new firmware and models. If your vehicle isn't listed, [contact us](mailto:info@stroomlijnen.nl).
:::

## Passenger Cars (CCS DC)

While Stroomlijnen chargers are designed for commercial vehicles, passenger cars with CCS connectors can charge too. PnC support:

| Manufacturer | PnC Support | Notes |
|-------------|:-----------:|-------|
| **BMW** | ✅ | i4, iX, i5, i7 — via BMW Charging |
| **Mercedes-Benz** | ✅ | EQS, EQE, EQA, EQB — via Mercedes me Charge |
| **Porsche** | ✅ | Taycan, Macan Electric — via Porsche Charging Service |
| **Audi** | ✅ | e-tron GT, Q6 e-tron, Q8 e-tron — via myAudi |
| **VW** | ⚠️ | ID. series — via Volkswagen We Charge, rollout ongoing |
| **Hyundai/Kia** | ⚠️ | Ioniq 5/6, EV6/9 — PnC varies by market and firmware |
| **Tesla** | ❌ | Uses proprietary Supercharger PnC, CCS PnC not supported |
| **Stellantis brands** | ❌ | No ISO 15118 PnC on current models |

:::tip
Even without PnC, all vehicles with a **CCS Combo 2** connector can charge at Stroomlijnen locations using an RFID card or the Stroomlijnen app. PnC simply removes the need for manual authentication.
:::

## How to Check Your Vehicle

1. **Check your OEM portal** — Volvo Connect, DAF Connect, Fleetboard, RIO, or Scania Fleet Management should show "Plug & Charge" or "ISO 15118" settings
2. **Check firmware version** — Ensure your vehicle is running the latest firmware (see [brand-specific guides](./brand-guides/volvo-trucks))
3. **Contact your dealer** — They can verify ISO 15118 capability and install certificates if needed
4. **Contact Stroomlijnen** — We can check compatibility when you register in our portal

## Missing Your Vehicle?

If your truck or van isn't listed, it may still work. New models and firmware updates are released regularly. Contact us at [info@stroomlijnen.nl](mailto:info@stroomlijnen.nl) with your vehicle make, model, and VIN, and we'll investigate.
