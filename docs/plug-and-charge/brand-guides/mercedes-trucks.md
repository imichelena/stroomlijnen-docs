# Mercedes-Benz Trucks — Plug & Charge Setup

Mercedes-Benz (Daimler Truck) supports ISO 15118-2 Plug & Charge on the eActros 600 and eActros 300. The eEconic is expected to receive PnC support via OTA update in Q2 2026. Configuration is handled through the Fleetboard platform.

## Supported Models

| Model | Battery Options | Max DC Charging | PnC Ready |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|:---------:|
| eActros 600 | Up to 621 kWh | 400 kW (MCS/CCS) | ✅ |
| eActros 300 | Up to 336 kWh | 160 kW (CCS) | ✅ |
| eEconic | Up to 336 kWh | 160 kW (CCS) | ⚠️ Q2 2026 |

## Prerequisites

- Fleetboard account with fleet management admin access
- eActros firmware with eMobility module (shipped standard since Q4 2025)
- Stroomlijnen fleet account with PnC enabled ([Setup Guide](../setup-guide))

## Step-by-Step: Fleetboard

### 1. Access eMobility Settings

1. Log in to [Fleetboard](https://fleetboard.com)
2. Navigate to **eMobility** → **Charging Management**
3. If you're using the new **Daimler Truck Digital** portal, go to **Services** → **Charge Management**

### 2. Add Stroomlijnen as Provider

1. In Charging Management, click **Charging Providers**
2. Click **Add Provider**
3. Search for **"Stroomlijnen B.V."** (or use EMSP ID `NL-STM`)
4. Click **Connect**
5. Accept the data sharing terms (vehicle ID and certificate exchange)

### 3. Register Vehicles for PnC

1. Go to **Charging Management** → **Vehicle PnC Settings**
2. For each eActros you want to enable:
   - Click the vehicle row
   - Toggle **Plug & Charge** to ON
   - Select **Stroomlijnen** as the preferred charging provider (or one of multiple)
3. Click **Save Changes**

### 4. Certificate Deployment

After saving:
1. Fleetboard initiates the certificate request with Stroomlijnen
2. The contract certificate is generated by our backend
3. It's pushed to the truck via the Daimler Truck telematics unit
4. **Typical deployment time: 2–6 hours**
5. The truck must be online (ignition on or connected to charging)

### 5. Verify in Both Portals

- **Fleetboard** → eMobility → Vehicle → PnC Status: should show "Certificate Active"
- **Stroomlijnen My Fleet** → Vehicle → Plug & Charge: should show "✅ Active"

### 6. First Session

Connect the eActros to a Stroomlijnen charger. The eActros dashboard will show "Plug & Charge" authentication status. Charging should start automatically within 5–10 seconds.

## Multiple Charging Providers

Mercedes-Benz supports multiple PnC providers simultaneously. You can have Stroomlijnen certificates alongside other provider certificates on the same vehicle. The truck will use the appropriate certificate based on the charger's network.

## Fleetboard vs. Daimler Truck Digital

Daimler Truck is transitioning from Fleetboard to the new **Daimler Truck Digital** platform. Both portals currently support PnC management:

- **Fleetboard (legacy):** Full PnC support, well-documented
- **Daimler Truck Digital (new):** PnC available under Services → Charge Management

If you're migrating between platforms, PnC settings will carry over automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### "eMobility" section not available in Fleetboard
- Ensure your Fleetboard subscription includes the eMobility module
- The eMobility module is included in Fleetboard Premium for electric vehicles
- Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer to verify

### Certificate shows "Pending" for extended period
1. Ensure the truck has been online for at least 2 hours
2. Check Fleetboard → Notifications for any pending approvals
3. Verify that the Daimler Truck telematics unit has mobile network connectivity (check antenna in truck)
4. Contact Stroomlijnen support for backend verification

### eActros 600 with MCS connector
The eActros 600 supports both CCS and MCS (Megawatt Charging System). PnC works on both connector types at Stroomlijnen locations. MCS chargers are being deployed at select sites in 2026.
