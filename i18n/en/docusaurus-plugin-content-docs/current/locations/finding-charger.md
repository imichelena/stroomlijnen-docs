# Finding a charging location

Our charging locations are at sites where we have installed them together with the owner: theatres and cultural institutions, hotels and businesses. They are not all publicly accessible — some are there for the own fleet or for guests.

## Where to find the current list

| Source | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **[stroomlijnen.nl](https://stroomlijnen.nl)** | The sites that are publicly accessible, with address |
| **The Customer Portal** | All sites you have access to, with live status per charging point |
| **Your account manager** | Sites in preparation, and access for specific registration numbers |

:::note Not yet on public maps
Our charging points are not yet visible everywhere in apps such as Chargemap or PlugShare. Supplying charging point data to the National Road Traffic Data Database (NDW) — mandatory under the European AFIR regulation — is in preparation. Until then: use the list on our site or ask. There is no separate Stroomlijnen map app; if you see one mentioned elsewhere, that is no longer correct.
:::

## Access

Depending on the site:

- **Freely accessible** — you can charge there without an appointment
- **By appointment or with a code** — arranged in advance with the site manager
- **Closed** — for the own fleet or guests only; not usable by third parties

In doubt? Ask before you drive there, rather than standing there with a full schedule.

## What you will find at a site

See the [Site guide](/en/docs/locations/site-guide) for the layout, the operation and the safety provisions.

## Roaming

With a charge card from another provider you can generally **not** charge with us: roaming is deliberately switched off on our chargers because of the costs. On request, a direct link is possible. See [Roaming explained](/en/docs/pandeneigenaren/cpo-roaming-uitgelegd).

That does not mean you cannot charge anywhere else — it means that with us you need an account or a card from us.

## Charging en route (outside our network)

For journeys that fall outside our sites, there are national and European networks. Watch out for two things that are often surprising:

- **Lorries cannot go everywhere.** Many fast chargers are designed for passenger cars: too tight, cable too low, no drive-through option. Look for lorry charging plazas, not the first fast charger you come across.
- **Prices differ greatly** between networks and between payment methods. Anyone charging with a third-party card often pays more than the price on the charger.
