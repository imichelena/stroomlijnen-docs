# Charging etiquette

Stroomlijnen chargers are shared facilities. By observing a few simple conventions, the experience stays good for everyone.

## The Golden Rules

### 1. Do Not Block the Charger
As soon as your vehicle is sufficiently charged, move it. Other vehicles may be waiting. If you charge overnight, that is fine — the bays are designed for that. But be alert during peak hours.

### 2. Disconnect When Done
When your session is complete (target SoC reached or you need to leave):
1. End the session
2. Hang the cable back neatly in the holder
3. Drive your vehicle out of the charging bay

### 3. One Vehicle Per Bay
Do not park several vehicles in one charging bay, even if only one is charging. Each bay is designed for one vehicle.

### 4. Do Not Disconnect Others
Never disconnect the charging cable from another vehicle. If all charging points are occupied, wait or coordinate with the site manager. The Customer Portal shows active sessions of your own fleet, not those of others.

### 5. Keep It Clean
- Do not leave waste behind in the charging area
- Report spilled liquids or hazards
- Hang cables back in their holders — do not leave them lying on the ground

## For Fleet Drivers

### Communicate with Dispatch
If you stay at a charger for a longer period, let dispatch know so that they can send other vehicles to alternative locations if necessary.

### Report Problems
If you come across a faulty charger, a dirty site or a safety issue — report it. Your feedback helps us maintain the network for everyone.

### Respect the Site
At theatre sites you are a guest on someone else's premises. Be respectful towards theatre staff, visitors and the surroundings. Stroomlijnen's reputation depends on good relationships with site owners.

## Peak hours

Busyness depends strongly on the site. At most places:

- **Morning** — vehicles that are leaving want a top-up
- **Late afternoon / evening** — vehicles return, and at sites with a building peak (theatre, hotel, restaurant) less power is available for charging at that time

Charging outside those windows often delivers more power. At sites with an energy budget, it can also mean that you stay within your budget without a session becoming slower.
