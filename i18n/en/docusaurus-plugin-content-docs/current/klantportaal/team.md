# Team and roles

Charging data is company data. So do not give people more access than they need.

## The roles

There are essentially three levels. The exact naming inside the portal may differ; what matters is what someone is *allowed* to do.

| Level | Can see and do | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Everything: fleet, energy, sessions, tariffs, invoices, users and account details | Management, facility manager |
| **User** | Fleet, energy, sessions, tariffs and invoices — no user management or account details | Operations manager, controller |
| **Driver** | Only their own sessions | Driving staff |

Read-only access without change rights is arranged as a user with view permissions only; we agree that per account.

## Adding someone

1. Open the user management section in the Customer Portal (your account manager will point you to it).
2. Enter the email address and the role.
3. The invitee receives a message and sets a password themselves.

Access is tied to the email address. Preferably use a business address that stays with the organisation, not the personal address of an employee who may leave later.

## Revoking access

On departure or a change of role, in this order:

1. **Team** — set the user to inactive or remove them.
2. **Vehicles** — block charge cards linked to that person.
3. Check whether there are **email addresses for invoices** that still go to that person.

Step 2 is often forgotten. An employee who leaves with a card in their bag can keep charging until that card is blocked. See [RFID cards](/en/docs/getting-started/rfid-cards).

## Recommendations

- **At least one additional owner.** With a single owner, one person leaving the company is all it takes to have a blocked account.
- **Viewer for the controller**, not owner. Anyone who only needs to look on does not need change permissions.
- **No shared login details.** If several people use the same account, it is impossible to trace afterwards who did what.
- **Clean up annually.** Go through the user list once a year.

## Passwords

Passwords are managed by you via the login page. We cannot view passwords. If you suspect misuse: change the password and inform us, so that we can review the session history.
