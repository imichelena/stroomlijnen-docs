# Invoicing

You pay for **energy**, not for sessions or time. That is the entire tariff model, and it is deliberately simple: one tariff per kWh per site, laid down in your agreement.

## What the invoice consists of

| Item | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Energy** | Charged kWh × the agreed tariff per site |
| **VAT** | 21%, stated separately |
| **Total** | Amount payable |

There are **no start-up costs per session** and **no time-based costs**. If they do appear, something is wrong — report it.

## What is included

The invoice comes with the session details, so that you can check the figures:

- date and time per session
- site and charging point
- vehicle (registration number and/or VIN)
- kWh delivered
- tariff and amount
- authentication method

You can also filter and export those details yourself under **Sessions**. See [Sessions](/en/docs/klantportaal/sessions).

## Tariffs

The tariff is shown per site in the portal under **Tariffs**, with its breakdown. See [Tariff structure](/en/docs/klantportaal/tarieven).

We agree tariff changes with you in advance. A tariff on the invoice that differs from the one in the portal is an error — not something to correct yourself in your records.

:::note Why no amounts are listed here
Tariffs depend on your site, your contract and the year. That is why no kWh prices are given in this knowledge base: they age faster than the page. You will find your current tariff in the portal and in your agreement.
:::

## Payment arrangements

The payment term, payment method (direct debit or bank transfer) and the details you need for it are set out in your agreement and on the invoice itself. Invoices can be downloaded as PDF; they are not sent by post.

## Checking the invoice

In three steps:

1. **Overall picture** — does the number of kWh match what you expect based on usage?
2. **Look for an outlier** — sort the sessions by kWh and look at the highest. Does the duration make sense?
3. **Recalculate one session** — kWh × tariff should give the session amount.

If anything deviates, report it with the invoice number and the session(s) concerned.

## In the event of a dispute

1. Report it within the payment term, with the invoice number and the session(s) concerned.
2. We investigate using the session data and the meter readings.
3. If the invoice is incorrect, a credit note follows or a correction on the next invoice.

You do not have to pay a disputed invoice first in order for it to be handled; just let us know before the due date.

## Accounting

- Invoices carry the details your accounting needs (amounts excluding and including VAT)
- The CSV export of sessions can be used for cost centres and management reporting
- For structural links with an ERP: see [Integrations and data export](/en/docs/technical/integration)
