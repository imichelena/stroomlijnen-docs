# The Customer Portal

The Customer Portal is the web environment in which you can see what is happening at your site(s) and where you manage your fleet and costs.

**Address:** [portal.stroomlijnen.nl](https://portal.stroomlijnen.nl)

You receive access during onboarding; there is no self-registration. That way we prevent data from one organisation ending up with another.

:::note About the section names
The layout of the portal changes over time and can differ per account. The names below are descriptive, not exact button labels. Sign in at [portal.stroomlijnen.nl](https://portal.stroomlijnen.nl) for the current layout, or ask your account manager.
:::

## The sections

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Live status of the chargers, what has been charged today, and the key figures |
| **Energy** | Consumption, generation, battery and grid in one view — including the power currently available |
| **Sessions** | All charging sessions, filterable, with details and export |
| **Vehicles** | Your vehicles and the charge cards linked to them |
| **Tariffs** | The tariff per site and how it is built up |
| **Invoices** | Your monthly invoices, with session details, as PDF |
| **Team** | Who has access and with which role |
| **Account** | Company details, contract and password |

## Roles

Not everyone needs to see everything. The roles are:

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including team, account and tariffs |
| **Manager** | Fleet, sessions, energy and reporting — no team management or account details |
| **Viewer** | Read-only: dashboard, sessions, energy |
| **Driver** | Only their own sessions via the app, no portal access |

You manage roles under **Team**. See [Team and roles](/en/docs/klantportaal/team).

## Multiple sites

If you manage more than one site, a site selector appears in the navigation. You see the current status of the chargers per site and can filter per site. Some sections are site-specific (energy, tariffs), others apply to the whole organisation (invoices, team).

## What the portal is *not*

- **Not a charge card app.** Drivers use the QR functionality or a card; the portal is for management.
- **Not real-time control of the building.** You can see what the EMS is doing and adjust within the agreed margins, but you cannot override the safety limits.
- **Not a public API.** See [Integrations and data export](/en/docs/technical/integration).

## Logging in

- Forgotten your password? Use the link on the login page.
- Two-factor authentication and other account set-up are discussed during onboarding.
- If something does not work, report it via your account manager or [stroomlijnen.nl/contact](https://stroomlijnen.nl/contact).
