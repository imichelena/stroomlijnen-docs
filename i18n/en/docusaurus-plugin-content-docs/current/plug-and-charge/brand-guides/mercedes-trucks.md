# Mercedes-Benz Trucks — Enabling Plug & Charge

Mercedes-Benz (Daimler Truck) supports ISO 15118-2 Plug & Charge on the eActros 600 and eActros 300. The eEconic is expected to receive PnC support via an OTA update in Q2 2026. Configuration is managed through the Fleetboard platform.

## Supported Models

| Model | Battery Options | Max. DC Charging | PnC Ready |
|-------|-----------|---------------|:---------:|
| eActros 600 | Up to 621 kWh | 400 kW (MCS/CCS) | ✅ |
| eActros 300 | Up to 336 kWh | 160 kW (CCS) | ✅ |
| eEconic | Up to 336 kWh | 160 kW (CCS) | ⚠️ Q2 2026 |

## Requirements

- Fleetboard account with administrator access for fleet management
- eActros firmware with eMobility module (standard since Q4 2025)
- Stroomlijnen fleet account with PnC enabled ([Setup Guide](../setup-guide))

## Step-by-Step: Fleetboard

### 1. Accessing eMobility Settings

1. Log in to [Fleetboard](https://fleetboard.com)
2. Navigate to **eMobility** → **Charging Management**
3. If using the new **Daimler Truck Digital** portal, go to **Services** → **Charging Management**

### 2. Adding Stroomlijnen as a Provider

1. Click on **Charging Providers** in Charging Management
2. Click on **Add Provider**
3. Search for **"Stroomlijnen B.V."** (or use eMSP-ID `NL-STM`)
4. Click on **Connect**
5. Accept data sharing terms (vehicle ID and certificate exchange)

### 3. Registering Vehicles for PnC

1. Go to **Charging Management** → **Vehicle PnC Settings**
2. Do the following for each eActros you want to enable:
   - Click on the vehicle row
   - Set **Plug & Charge** to ON
   - Select **Stroomlijnen** as preferred charging provider (or one of multiple)
3. Click on **Save Changes**

### 4. Certificate Rollout

After saving:
1. Fleetboard initiates the certificate request to Stroomlijnen
2. The contract certificate is generated by our backend
3. It is pushed to the truck via Daimler Truck's telematics unit
4. **Typical rollout time: 2–6 hours**
5. The truck must be online (ignition on or connected to a charger)

### 5. Verify in Both Portals

- **Fleetboard** → eMobility → Vehicle → PnC status: should display "Certificate Active"
- **Stroomlijnen Fleet** → Vehicle → Plug & Charge: should display "✅ Active"

### 6. First Session

Connect the eActros to a Stroomlijnen charger. The eActros dashboard displays the authentication status "Plug & Charge". Charging should start automatically within 5–10 seconds.

## Multiple Charging Providers

Mercedes-Benz supports multiple PnC providers simultaneously. You can have Stroomlijnen certificates alongside certificates from other providers on the same vehicle. The truck uses the correct certificate based on the charger's network.

## Fleetboard versus Daimler Truck Digital

Daimler Truck is transitioning from Fleetboard to the new **Daimler Truck Digital** platform. Both portals currently support PnC management:

- **Fleetboard (legacy):** Full PnC support, well-documented
- **Daimler Truck Digital (new):** PnC available under Services → Charging Management

If you migrate between platforms, PnC settings are automatically transferred.

## Troubleshooting

### eMobility Section Not Available in Fleetboard
- Ensure your Fleetboard subscription includes the eMobility module
- The eMobility module is included in Fleetboard Premium for electric vehicles
- Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer for verification

### Certificate Shows "In Progress" for an Extended Period
1. Ensure the truck has been online for at least 2 hours
2. Check Fleetboard → Notifications for any pending approvals
3. Verify Daimler Truck's telematics unit has mobile network connection (check antenna in truck)
4. Contact Stroomlijnen support for backend verification

### eActros 600 with MCS Connector
The eActros 600 supports both CCS and MCS (Megawatt Charging System). PnC works on both connector types at Stroomlijnen locations. MCS chargers will be deployed at select locations in 2026.
