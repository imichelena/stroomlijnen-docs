# Troubleshooting

Distinguish **enrolment**, **approval** and **charging**. A recorded EVCCID (the MAC address of the vehicle communication controller) does not mean the account owner has already approved it or that charging is possible in every circumstance. Entering a VIN does not solve this.

## Enrolment fails

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Card is not read | First press **“Hold your card to the reader”**, then present the card. Simply opening the screen does not activate the reader. |
| Unknown card | Use a card linked to your account. After **“Try again”**, activate the reader again with the button. |
| Reader or platform unavailable | Follow the error message; try later if the platform is unreachable. |
| No RDW details | Choose **“Continue”** to proceed without RDW confirmation; correct an incorrect registration number. |
| No vehicle found | Connect only when prompted and check that the cable is seated correctly. The vehicle must supply a usable EVCCID. |
| Time expired | Start again. The total 5 minutes run from **Begin**; capture can take up to 3 minutes, but not beyond the overall deadline. |
| Recording failed | Capturing the identity alone does not prove registration. Follow the **“Not completed”** message and the offered retry option. |
| Confirmation email failed | Read whether the vehicle was still recorded. An email error does not automatically mean registration failed. Ask the account owner to check the registration. |

See [Setup](/en/docs/plug-and-charge/setup-guide) for the full sequence. Rental and registration-number steps may vary by configuration.

## The session does not start automatically

- **Not yet enrolled:** use enrolment at a suitable charger; portal details alone are not enough.
- **Waiting for the account owner:** ask the account owner to check permission. Confirmation of recording is not approval.
- **Denied or expired:** ask the account owner to review this; enrolling again does not bypass a denial or end date.
- **Recognition missing:** have the EVCCID link and vehicle communication checked. Note whether it worked previously and whether vehicle software changed.
- **Charging not permitted or not possible:** check the display message, charging permissions, vehicle settings and conditions at the site.

A card or QR option may be an alternative if the charger offers it and you are authorised to use it. This is no guarantee that a session will start.

## Safe checks

Follow the vehicle and charger instructions. Do not force or disconnect a locked connector. Do not use a visibly damaged cable or connector; report the problem. A charging stop may also be caused by the vehicle, battery or charger, not just recognition.

## What to report

Provide the registration number, make/model, site, charging-point number, date and time. Copy the screen message exactly or take a photo without unnecessary personal data. State whether the problem occurs during enrolment, approval or charging and whether it worked previously.

Report this through your account manager or [stroomlijnen.nl/contact](https://stroomlijnen.nl/contact). For an urgent problem, use the contact number on the charger.
